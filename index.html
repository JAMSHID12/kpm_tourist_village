About KPM

KPM Tourist Village History

KPM Tourist Village was born out of a vision — to transform an abandoned rock quarry into a vibrant eco-retreat that celebrates nature and local heritage. Today, it stands as a serene destination where guests can unwind, explore, and reconnect with the simple joys of life.

What Makes Us Unique
  • Built in an Old Rock Quarry: A creative reuse of natural landscape
  • Eco-Tourism Focus: Minimal impact, maximum experience
  • Family-Friendly Spaces: Safe, peaceful, and beautifully maintained

At KPM Tourist Village, we believe in creating experiences that bring people closer — to nature, to each other, and to themselves.

Experience Nature’s Best:

  • Walk through lush gardens and scenic trails
  • Watch colorful koi fish gliding gracefully in our pond
  • Enjoy peaceful moments surrounded by hills and trees

Programs & Activities at KPM

Experience the Beauty of Gardens & Koi Ponds

Step into our landscaped gardens, where every flower and tree adds to the charm of the surroundings. Don’t miss our koi pond, home to hundreds of vibrant, friendly koi fish that bring life and color to the water.

Coracle Boat Ride – With a Magical Twist

Take a gentle coracle boat ride across the pond — feed the koi as they follow your boat in a beautiful, colorful dance. It’s an enchanting experience that delights children, families, and couples alike.

Dine With Nature

After exploring, enjoy a relaxing meal at our open-air restaurant. Our dishes are made with fresh, local ingredients and served in a calm, nature-filled ambiance — the perfect spot to recharge your senses.
